## Roadmap discipline
`ROADMAP.md` is the **coordination constitution** every session reads first: it
defines the work lanes, the file-ownership map, git-worktree isolation, and
the merge protocol. The actual backlog lives in the lane roadmaps:
- 🛠️ `ROADMAP-CORE.md` — Core / features lane (branch `core`).
- 🔒 `ROADMAP-SECURITY.md` — Security & Reliability lane (branch `security`).
- 🎨 `ROADMAP-DESIGN.md` — UX/UI Design lane (branch `design`); asset pack in `DESIGN.md`.

Before proposing or starting work: read `ROADMAP.md`, identify your lane, then
read **only your lane's roadmap**. After completing or re-scoping anything, update
that lane's changelog in the same change. Do not plan from memory or from
`docs/EDG3-Roadmap.xlsx` — that spreadsheet is deprecated.
