## Roadmap discipline
`ROADMAP.md` is the single source of truth for priorities and status. Before
proposing or starting work, read it. After completing or re-scoping anything,
update it (and its changelog) in the same change. Do not plan from memory or
from `docs/EDG3-Roadmap.xlsx` — that spreadsheet is deprecated.
