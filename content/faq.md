## Getting started

**What is Edge?**
Edge is your AI chief of staff. Every morning, he calls you, tells you how your day looks, and reshapes your calendar around the things that matter most — your three areas of focus and your energy level. The call takes 3–5 minutes. Your week gets better. That's the loop.

**What do I need to get started?**
Just a Google Calendar and a phone number. That's it. Everything else — Gmail, Whoop — makes Edge smarter, but nothing is required beyond your calendar.

**Do I need a smartphone app?**
No. Edge calls you — you don't call Edge. Your dashboard is a web app you can access from any browser. No app download required.

**Do I need a Whoop?**
No. Whoop unlocks automatic energy tracking (Edge reads your recovery and sleep scores), but if you don't have one, Edge asks about your energy at the start of each call instead. Red, yellow, or green — that's all he needs.

**How long does setup take?**
About 5 minutes. Connect your calendar, set your three focus areas, pick your call time. Edge handles the rest.

## How it works

**What happens on the morning call?**
Edge calls you at your chosen time, Monday through Friday. He opens with your Edge Score — a quick readout of how set up you are for a focused, energized day — then walks you through what needs attention, proposes changes to your calendar, and makes them when you say yes. The whole thing is a conversation. No menus, no tapping, just talk.

**What are the three areas of focus?**
Your three areas of focus are the things that matter most to you right now. Not your task list — the bigger categories your work and life are organized around. Examples: "grow my business," "get healthier," "improve my finances." Edge builds your entire calendar strategy around these three things.

You pick them during setup, and Edge will check in if they seem stale. You can update them anytime in your dashboard.

**How does Edge reshape my calendar?**
When Edge identifies a problem — a focus area with no scheduled time, a high-demand meeting sitting in your lowest-energy window, a day that's overloaded — he tells you what he'd change and why. You say yes (or no, or "do it differently"), and Edge makes the change directly in your Google Calendar. It's your calendar; Edge just helps manage it better.

**Can Edge create, move, or delete events?**
Yes — but only when you approve it. Edge never modifies your calendar without your explicit go-ahead in the conversation. You can also undo any change Edge makes, in the Activity tab of your dashboard.

**Can I talk to Edge outside the morning call?**
The morning call is the primary interaction. If you need to make a calendar change mid-day, you can do it directly in Google Calendar as you normally would.

**What if I miss my morning call?**
Edge won't leave a voicemail or reschedule automatically. You'll see a summary of what he would have covered in your dashboard under Briefings. Your call resumes the next morning at your regular time.

## Your data and privacy

**What does Edge access — and why?**

*Google Calendar (required):* Edge reads your events to understand your schedule and writes changes when you approve them. This is the foundation of everything Edge does.

*Gmail (optional):* Edge scans your inbox for signals that matter — urgent threads, financial or legal notices, replies from people you've contacted. It does not read newsletters, promotions, or automated emails, and it does not store the content of your emails. It looks for signals and discards the rest.

*Whoop (optional):* Edge reads your daily recovery score and sleep data to understand your energy level. This is read-only — Edge never writes anything to Whoop.

**Is my data encrypted?**
Yes. Your Google Calendar access credentials, Whoop health tokens, call transcripts, and stored facts are all encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM. Calendar events themselves are fetched live from Google — they are not stored in our database.

**Do you sell my data?**
No. Never. Your data is used to make Edge work for you — that's it. It is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used to train models for other users.

**Do you share my data with anyone?**
Edge uses third-party services to function (Google APIs, Vapi for calls, Anthropic for AI). These services process your data as needed to deliver the product, under their own privacy policies. We do not share your data with anyone else.

**Does Edge read every email I receive?**
No. Edge scans for specific signals — urgent sender patterns, financial or legal keywords, replies to threads you've been part of. It does not read the full body of emails, does not retain email content, and does not process newsletters, promotions, or anything flagged as non-personal by Gmail.

**Can I see what Edge knows about me?**
Yes. Your dashboard has a "What Edge knows" tab that shows everything Edge has learned — your focus areas, preferences, and facts from your calls. Nothing is hidden.

**Can I delete my data?**
Yes, at any time. Go to Settings → Account → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your profile, call history, calendar data, email signals, and all stored facts. The deletion is immediate and irreversible.

**Can I disconnect Google or Whoop without deleting my account?**
Yes. Go to Settings → Connections and disconnect any source at any time. Edge will stop accessing that source immediately. Reconnecting later is always an option.

**Who can see my data?**
Your data is yours. The Edge team does not access individual user data except when needed to diagnose a technical issue — and only with your permission. Call transcripts are stored encrypted and accessible only to you.

## The Edge Score

**What is the Edge Score?**
It's a daily number (0–100) that tells you how set up you are to do your best work today. Not how busy you are — how well-positioned you are. Think of it like a credit score for your day: one number, with a breakdown showing exactly what's driving it and what to change.

**What are the four components?**

*Focus (30%)* — Does your calendar reflect your three areas of focus? A high Focus score means your priorities have protected time. A low score means your calendar is full of everything except what matters most.

*Energy (30%)* — How's your capacity today? Informed by your Whoop data (sleep + recovery) or your self-reported check-in at the start of your call. Edge uses this to know whether to push or protect your schedule.

*Clarity (20%)* — How well does Edge know you? The more you've shared — focus areas, energy profile, connected tools, call history — the smarter Edge gets. Clarity grows as the relationship deepens.

*Momentum (20%)* — Are you showing up consistently? Completed morning calls, confirmed focus plans, consistent engagement. Momentum compounds: small, daily actions build the score and the results.

**Why is my score low or showing "calibrating"?**
"Calibrating" means Edge doesn't have enough data yet to give you a meaningful number — usually in the first few days. Keep showing up for your morning calls and the score will stabilize. A low score after calibration is honest signal: it means something specific is off, and Edge can tell you exactly what.

**Can I raise my Edge Score?**
Yes — and that's the point. The score is designed to be actionable. Each component has a clear driver and a clear fix. Edge will tell you what's dragging it down and propose specific changes. After you apply them, the score updates.

## Your morning calls

**When does Edge call me?**
At the time you set during onboarding, Monday through Friday. The default is 9:00 AM in your local timezone.

**Can I change my call time?**
Yes, anytime. Go to Settings → Call Time and update it. The change takes effect the next business day.

**What if I'm in a different timezone when traveling?**
Edge calls based on the timezone you set in your profile. If you're traveling, update your timezone in Settings before you go — or mention it to Edge on a call and he'll note it.

**What number does Edge call from?**
A consistent number associated with your account. You may want to save it as "Edge" in your contacts so you know to pick up.

**How long is a typical call?**
3–5 minutes. Some calls run a little longer if there's more to discuss or more calendar changes to make. Edge keeps it tight — you have a day to get to.

**Can I skip a day?**
Yes. Just don't pick up. Your score and dashboard update regardless. If you're going on vacation or want to pause calls temporarily, you can do that in Settings → Pause calls.

## Your account

**How do I reconnect Google if something breaks?**
Go to Settings → Connections → Google → Reconnect. This refreshes the permissions without affecting your data or history.

**Why does Edge ask me to reconnect Google?**
Usually because your Google access token has expired or a new scope is needed (like Gmail). Reconnecting takes 30 seconds and restores full functionality.

**Can I add a second Google account?**
Not currently. Edge works with one Google account per user.

**How do I cancel?**
Go to Settings → Account → Cancel subscription. Your access continues until the end of your current billing period. Your data is retained for 30 days after cancellation in case you want to come back, then permanently deleted.

**I found a bug or something isn't working — what do I do?**
Email us at support@edg3.ai or mention it to Edge on your next call. Beta users get direct access to the team — your feedback directly shapes what gets built next.
