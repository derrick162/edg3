# Edge — Beta Onboarding Email Sequence
_Pairs with Cam's in-app first-run flow. Send from derrick@deltaedg3.com — personal tone, not marketing._

---

## Email 1 — You're in (send immediately on account creation)

**Subject:** you're in — here's what to expect

Hey [NAME],

You're set up on Edge. Here's what happens next.

**Tomorrow morning, Edge will call you.** At [CALL TIME] — the time you set during onboarding. The first call will feel a little rough. Edge doesn't know you yet, and he'll ask more questions than usual. That's normal. Give it 3–5 calls before you judge it.

**What the call looks like:**
- Edge opens with your Edge Score and what's driving it
- He walks through what needs attention today
- He proposes specific calendar changes — you say yes, no, or adjust
- The whole thing takes 3–5 minutes

**One thing to do before tomorrow:** make sure your three areas of focus actually reflect what matters to you right now. If they're off, your first few calls will be off. Takes 2 minutes in your dashboard → Priorities.

If anything breaks, reply here. I'll fix it personally.

Derrick

---

## Email 2 — After first call (send day 2, or trigger on first call completion)

**Subject:** how'd the first call go?

Hey [NAME],

You've had your first call with Edge. I want to know how it felt.

Not "was it perfect" — it won't be yet. More: did it feel like it was tracking what actually matters to you? Did the calendar suggestions make sense? Did anything feel off or weird?

The feedback you give me in the first week shapes what gets built next. So don't be polite — be specific.

One thing worth knowing: **Edge gets sharper as it learns you.** The memory from each call feeds into the next one. By week 2, it should feel noticeably more like it knows how you work.

Reply here or just tell Edge on your next call — he'll flag it.

Derrick

---

## Email 3 — End of week 1 (send day 7)

**Subject:** week 1 check-in

Hey [NAME],

One week in. A few things I'm curious about:

1. Are you actually picking up the calls? (No judgment — if you're not, I want to know why.)
2. Has Edge said anything that surprised you — in a good or bad way?
3. What's one thing that would make it 10x more useful?

Also: your Edge Score this week — is it tracking something real, or does it feel disconnected from how you're actually doing?

I'm using this feedback directly. Not filtering it through a survey or a product team. Just me reading your replies.

Derrick

---

## Email 4 — End of week 2 (send day 14)

**Subject:** two weeks with Edge — honest read?

Hey [NAME],

Two weeks. You've had enough calls now that Edge has a real read on how you work.

I want an honest take: is this tool actually changing how your days go? Even slightly?

If yes — what specifically? If no — what's missing?

I'm also going to ask you something I ask all my design partners at this point: **who else should be using this?** Not because I want you to do my marketing — because if you've gotten value from it, there's probably someone in your network who'd get more.

If you think of someone, just reply with their name. I'll reach out directly and mention you sent me.

Derrick

---

## Ongoing — monthly check-in (send day 30, 60, 90)

**Subject:** [Month] check-in — what's Edge getting right/wrong?

Hey [NAME],

[Month] check-in. Three questions:

1. What's working well that I should protect?
2. What's most broken or annoying?
3. What's one thing you wish Edge could do that it can't yet?

Takes 2 minutes to reply. Genuinely shapes the roadmap.

Derrick
