## Draft A — The honest version (recommended)

Two years ago I burned out.

Not "tired and stressed" burned out. The real kind — where you wake up every morning and the first feeling is dread, and you genuinely can't remember why you started any of it.

I had built Cardify/Drop to $100M raised. Built and led a real team. And then watched it go into receivership. Lost the company, the team, a lot of the identity I'd wrapped around it.

I spent a year trying to figure out what happened. And one of the things I kept coming back to: I never had a chief of staff.

Not a task manager. Not a productivity app. Someone who looks at your week and says: this is wrong, here's why, let me fix it. Someone who tracks your energy, not just your schedule. Someone who notices when you're about to run yourself into the ground and says something.

So I built one.

Edge is an AI chief of staff. Every morning, he calls you — takes about 5 minutes. He already knows your priorities, your energy, and how your calendar looks. He opens with a diagnosis and a proposal. You say yes. Your day gets better.

I've been using it on myself for months. It's the first tool I've ever built that I genuinely rely on. Not because it's my company — because it works.

We're opening access to a small group before public launch. If you're a founder, exec, or high-performer who feels like your weeks are getting away from you — especially if you have ADHD — I want to talk to you.
