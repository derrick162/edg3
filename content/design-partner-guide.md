## What Edge is (and isn't)

Edge is a voice AI that calls you every morning. It reads your calendar, tells you what to focus on, and reshapes your schedule around what actually matters. The call takes about 10 minutes.

**It is:**
- A focused daily ritual, not an always-on assistant
- Honest about what it knows and doesn't know
- Something that gets better the more you use it and tell it

**It isn't:**
- A personal assistant that handles email, travel, or admin
- Something that works in real-time between calls (your morning call is when things happen)
- Perfect — it will make mistakes; what matters is that you can see and fix them

---

## Day 0 — Setup

You'll get a link to sign up. Setup takes about 10 minutes:

1. Create your account
2. Connect your Google Calendar (required — this is the whole product)
3. Optionally connect Gmail (Edge reads subject lines to flag urgent threads; never message bodies)
4. Optionally connect Whoop (Edge uses your recovery score in its recommendations)
5. Tell Edge your top 3 focus areas — what you're most trying to make progress on right now
6. Set your call time — any time works, but most people do 7:00–8:30 AM

After setup, Edge will show you what it already knows from your calendar. That's your first sign the product is real — it knows something true about your week before you've had a single call.

## Day 1 — Your first call

Edge will call you at your set time. Pick up.

The call will go something like this:

> "Good morning — let's look at your week. You've got [N] events, including [one specific event that stands out]. On [focus area], you have no protected time yet — do you want to block some?"

Edge will ask for your input and offer to make changes. It's conversational — you talk, it responds.

**Tips for the first call:**
- Just talk. You don't need to memorize commands.
- Say yes to at least one thing Edge proposes. The first call is most useful when you let it do something.
- Don't worry about the call being perfect. It won't be. The second call will be better.

---

## What happens on the call

Edge does three things:

1. **Reads your calendar** — tells you what's happening this week, what looks off, what's missing
2. **Proposes changes** — offers to create a focus block, move a meeting, clean up duplicates. You approve before anything changes.
3. **Remembers what you say** — if you mention a goal, a person, a constraint, Edge stores it and uses it in future calls

After the call, you can check the **Activity tab** in your dashboard to see exactly what Edge did — every action it took is logged.

---

## The Edge Score

Your dashboard shows an Edge Score (0–100). It's made up of:
- **Focus (30%)** — how much of your scheduled time goes toward your stated priorities
- **Energy (30%)** — your recovery level (needs Whoop; shows "calibrating" otherwise)
- **Clarity (20%)** — how much Edge knows about you (fills in over the first few calls)
- **Momentum (20%)** — whether you're following through on your commitments

The score starts rough and gets more accurate over the first 2 weeks. If it doesn't feel right, **tell Edge on the next call** — it will adjust.

---

## What Edge knows about you

Edge stores facts it learns from your calls — your priorities, goals, important contacts, preferences. You can see all of them in the **"What Edge knows"** tab.

If something is wrong (and something will be wrong), you can:
- **Edit the fact** — click the pencil icon next to it
- **Delete the fact** — click the trash icon
- Edge won't argue

This tab is the most important trust signal in the product. Check it after your first few calls.

---

## When something breaks

It will. Here's what to do:

- **Edge didn't call:** Text Derrick directly. He'll investigate.
- **Edge did something to your calendar you didn't want:** Go to Activity tab → click Undo. It's one click.
- **Edge said something wrong or confused:** Tell it on the next call ("you said X but that's not right") — it will correct itself.
- **Something in the app is broken:** Email derrick@edg3.ai. He'll respond within a few hours.

## What we need from you

Three things:

1. **Show up for the call.** Edge's value compounds with daily use. The first week is when the habit forms. If you miss a call, reschedule it that day.
2. **Tell Edge when it's wrong.** This is how it gets better — for you and for the product.
3. **Tell Derrick what's useful and what's annoying.** A 2-sentence voice note or text is perfect. You don't need to wait for a formal check-in.
