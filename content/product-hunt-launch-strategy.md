# Product Hunt Launch Strategy
_Target: September 2026. Preparation starts August 1._
_Goal: Top 5 Product of the Day. Realistic expectation: 300–600 upvotes with good execution._

---

## What PH actually is (and isn't)

Product Hunt is a discovery platform for makers and early adopters. A good launch brings:
- 300–800 visitors in 24 hours
- 50–150 new waitlist/trial signups (at 15–20% conversion from visit to signup)
- Social proof: "Top 5 Product of the Day" is a credible line for the homepage
- Press pickup: a few journalists monitor PH daily for story pitches

It is NOT a growth engine on its own. It's a moment — a way to convert latent awareness in your network into action, and to give press something to write about.

**Don't launch before you're ready.** A mediocre PH launch is worse than none — you get the press attention without the conversion because the product doesn't land. Launch when: you have 2+ testimonials, a working product, and a clear "try it" CTA (waitlist or direct signup).

## Step 1 — Find a hunter

A "hunter" is a PH user who formally posts your product. A hunter with a large following gets your product visible to more people in the first hour (which drives the algorithm).

**What you want in a hunter:**
- Active PH user (has posted products before)
- Has followers on PH (500+)
- Knows your space or your story personally

**Where to find one:**
1. Your own network first — advisors, investors, other founders you know. Search them on Product Hunt to see if they have an account.
2. LinkedIn: search "Product Hunt" + your vertical. Many founders mention being active on PH.
3. Ask in communities: YC Startup School forum, IndieHackers, X — "looking for a PH hunter for AI productivity tool launch, who should I talk to?"

**What to say to a potential hunter:**
> "Hey [name] — I'm launching Edg3 on Product Hunt in September. It's an AI that calls founders every morning and keeps them connected to their priorities. I'm looking for a hunter with some PH presence. Would you be up for it? Takes 5 minutes to post — I'll prep everything for you."

If they say yes, you do all the work. They just click submit.

## Step 2 — Prepare your assets (4–6 hours total, done in August)

**Required:**
- **Thumbnail image** — 240x240px, visible at small size. Should show the product name prominently, not just a logo.
- **Gallery images** — 3–5 screenshots at 1270x952px. Show: (1) the dashboard/Edge Score, (2) the day plan card, (3) the morning call summary, (4) the priorities screen. Caption each.
- **Tagline** — 60 characters max. From `content/external-comms-kit.md`: "The AI that calls you every morning and keeps you focused on what matters."
- **Description** — already written in `content/external-comms-kit.md §Product Hunt`. Use verbatim; just update the launch date.

**Optional but high-impact:**
- **Demo video** — 60-90 second screencast showing a real call summary and a day plan. Narrate it. A video thumbnail gets 3x more clicks.
- **Maker photo** — your actual face. Not a logo. PH favors authentic founder stories.

## Step 3 — Seed 20–30 supporters before launch

This is the most important mechanic and the one most people miss. PH upvotes in the first 2 hours drive the algorithm. You need a list of people ready to upvote at 12:01 AM Pacific.

**Build the list (do this in August):**
- Every design partner (5)
- Every advisor (you have how many?)
- Founders in your network who would recognize the problem
- Anyone who's told you "that's a real problem" when you described Edg3
- Anyone on your waitlist who you've had a real conversation with

Target: 25–40 names. Not everyone will be awake at midnight Pacific, but if 15–20 show up in the first 2 hours, you're competitive.

**What to send them the day before launch:**
> "Hey [name] — launching Edg3 on Product Hunt tomorrow at midnight Pacific. If you've got 30 seconds, an upvote would mean a lot. I'll send the link tomorrow morning."

Short. Personal. No pressure language.

**What to send them on launch day (send at 7 AM Pacific — when most are awake):**
> "We're live on Product Hunt — [link]. If you've tried it or know someone this would resonate with, any support would mean a lot. Takes 30 seconds."

Note: PH discourages "upvote farming" (mass-emailing your whole list with explicit asks). The line is: personal outreach to people who know you or the product is fine. Mass-blast to cold contacts is against their guidelines and can get your launch flagged.

## Step 4 — The first comment

The maker's first comment is the most-read text on the page. It should tell your story in 200 words.

Draft (adapt to current state at launch time):

> Hi PH — I'm Derrick, and I built Edg3 after burning out building a $100M company.
>
> Here's what I learned: I wasn't bad at execution. I was bad at staying connected to what actually mattered while the day swallowed me. I'd spend weeks doing things that looked productive but weren't moving my priorities at all.
>
> Edg3 is my answer to that. Every morning, Edge calls you — voice call, 10 minutes — briefs you on your day, surfaces your priorities, and asks the uncomfortable questions about what you're avoiding. After the call, the dashboard shows a day plan tied to your actual calendar.
>
> We've been running a closed beta with 5 founders. [Insert current retention stat here, e.g. "4/5 are still taking daily calls at 30 days."]
>
> This is for founders and high-performers who know what they should be doing but keep losing themselves in the noise. Happy to answer anything.

Update the quote with your real retention stat at launch time. A specific number beats a vague claim every time.

---

## Step 5 — Launch day mechanics

**Midnight Pacific Tuesday:** hunter posts. You monitor.

**6 AM Pacific:** send supporter texts/messages with the link.

**8 AM–12 PM Pacific:** peak PH traffic. You respond to every single comment. Fast, personal replies drive engagement and keep you visible.

**When someone comments with a question or objection:** answer directly and honestly. "Does it work if my calendar isn't detailed?" — "Yes, Edge compensates by asking you about your week in the first call. The more you tell it, the more accurate it gets over time."

**When someone leaves a positive comment:** thank them personally. Don't be generic.

**Don't:** obsess over your rank every 5 minutes. Focus on conversation.

---

## What to do with a successful launch

**Top 5 Product of the Day:** add "Top 5 Product of the Day on Product Hunt" to the homepage. This is real social proof.

**Inbound waitlist signups from PH:** reach out personally to anyone who signed up with context ("I noticed you're [role] — curious what drew you to Edg3"). High-intent signups from PH are often the best early users.

**Press pickup:** if a journalist covers you (check for mentions the day after), respond quickly. A PH launch is a legitimate news hook — "AI startup gets [N] upvotes at launch" or "solo founder builds burnout-prevention AI."

**LinkedIn post:** day after launch, post your PH result with a brief story. "We launched on Product Hunt yesterday. [N] upvotes. More importantly, here's what the comments told us about what resonates..." — this gets 3-5x the engagement of a regular post.

## Realistic expectations

| Outcome | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Top 3 Product of the Day (500+ upvotes) | Breakout launch — rare, but earns major press | Send the journalist pitch from external-comms-kit immediately |
| Top 5–10 (200–400 upvotes) | Solid launch — adds credibility + 50-100 signups | Update homepage badge, follow up with all signups personally |
| Top 10–20 (100–200 upvotes) | Decent showing — add to outreach but not a headline | Still useful for social proof; focus on inbound quality |
| Below top 20 | Something went wrong (timing, no hunter network, product-copy mismatch) | Don't relaunch immediately; fix the issue (usually: wrong day, no hunter, listing copy off) |
