## The 3-question ICP triage

Ask these before going deep. Listen for which bucket they land in.

**Q1:** "Walk me through a day last week. What did you actually spend time on?"
- Heavy on meetings / reactive = **ICP 2 or 4**
- Context-switching chaos / can't focus = **ICP 1 or 3**
- Mentions energy/exhaustion unprompted = **ICP 2**

**Q2:** "What's the most expensive mistake you made with your time last month?"
- "Worked on the wrong thing" = **ICP 1, 3, or 4**
- "Said yes to too many things" = **ICP 2**
- "I don't really make mistakes like that" = probably not a fit

**Q3:** "If Edge didn't exist, what would be the next-best thing?"
- "Hire an EA" = **ICP 2 or 4** (high-value, premium buyer)
- "Try another app" = **ICP 1 or 3** (tool-switching history = we can win on simplicity)
- "I've just been living with it" = **hot lead — high pain, low discovery**

## ICP 1 — ADHD Founder/Operator ★ Beachhead

**Who:** 28–42, early-stage, ADHD (diagnosed or self-identified), tries many systems, none stick

**Pain:** Decision fatigue about what to work on. Time blindness. Context-switching. Open loops everywhere.

**Why Edge:** Does the deciding for them. Voice-first removes friction. Momentum creates accountability.

**Litmus question:** "Have you ever missed something important because of how your brain works?"
→ If they have a story: they're in.

**Trust threshold:** LOW — pain is acute, tries things quickly

**The yes:** Specific story about system failure + curiosity/relief when you describe the morning call

**The no:** Their main complaint is "not enough hours" (vs. "I can't control the hours I have")

---

## ICP 2 — Burnt-Out Senior Executive

**Who:** 35–50, growth-stage, 5–8h meetings/day, feels like lost control

**Pain:** Calendar is full of the wrong things. Depleted energy. Strategic drift. Personal priorities gone.

**Why Edge:** Hero loop as chief-of-staff substitute. Whoop integration for energy. Edge Score names the burnout.

**Litmus question:** "When was the last time you had a full day to actually think — not execute?"
→ If they struggle to remember: they're in.

**Trust threshold:** HIGH — needs to believe it before committing

**The yes:** Expresses "I've lost control" or "I'm not at my best" without being prompted

**The no:** Has a strong EA who handles the problem; or in denial about burnout ("just busy")

---

## ICP 3 — Early-Stage Solo Founder

**Who:** 25–38, 0–5 person team, context-switching hell, no planning mechanism

**Pain:** Context-switching cost. No system. Open-loop anxiety. Avoiding important things.

**Why Edge:** Open loops surfaced. Focus recommendation. Daily accountability ritual.

**Litmus question:** "How many things are in progress right now — half-started, unfinished, on a mental list?"
→ "Way too many" = they're in.

**Trust threshold:** LOW — willing to try; fast to abandon if first call disappoints

**The yes:** Open-loop anxiety + immediate openness to the morning call concept

**The no:** Heads-down in deep execution mode and doesn't want interruption

---

## ICP 4 — VC-Backed Founder (Series A–B)

**Who:** 30–45, 5–50 people, board pressure, strategic drift toward IC work

**Pain:** Doing execution when should be doing strategy. Calendar controls them. Runway anxiety.

**Why Edge:** Focus tied to runway. Hero loop for calendar hygiene. Edge Score as executive metric.

**Litmus question:** "When you look at last week — was that the highest-leverage thing you could have been doing?"
→ Pause + honest answer = they're in.

**Trust threshold:** MEDIUM — metrics-fluent, wants to see the data

**The yes:** Clear gap between what they intend and what they actually did

**The no:** Company running well, energy is high, no acute pain

## The "worth following up?" decision tree

```
Did they have a specific, emotional story about calendar/focus failure?
├── YES → Did they express openness to a morning call?
│         ├── YES → Hot lead. Send design-partner invite.
│         └── NO  → Warm lead. Follow up with the pitch one-pager.
└── NO  → Generic complaints only ("I'm just busy")?
          ├── YES → Not ready. Note and revisit in 3 months.
          └── NO  → They don't have the problem. Move on.
```

## Things to listen for (any ICP)

🟢 **Strong signals:**
- "I've tried everything and it doesn't work"
- "I know what I should do but I can't make myself do it"
- "My week just... gets away from me"
- A specific story about a missed deadline, missed event, or working on the wrong thing
- "I didn't think AI could actually be useful for this"

🔴 **Weak signals / wrong fit:**
- "I'm pretty organized, actually"
- "I have an EA who handles my calendar"
- "I just need a better task manager"
- "I don't want AI accessing my calendar" (without follow-up curiosity)
- They want something Edge can't do: travel, admin, hiring, logistics
