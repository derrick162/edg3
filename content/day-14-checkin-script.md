# Day-14 Check-in Interview Script
_20 minutes. Video or phone. Run at day 14 ± 2 days from first call._
_Goal: determine if this is becoming a habit, capture the first testimonial, and decide if this user is your day-30 retention signal._

This is your most data-rich conversation. Two weeks of daily calls means Edge knows their context. The question isn't whether the product worked in week 1 — it's whether the user is changing their behavior in week 2.

---

## Before the call (2 min prep)

Check:
- Total calls this user has had (Vapi dashboard) — is it tracking toward 4+/week?
- Did call frequency increase or decrease from week 1 to week 2?
- Any day plan applies in week 2? (A second week of applies means habit forming)
- Any bugs or trust breaks reported?
- What did you learn on day-7? What open questions did you note then?

If they've had 8+ calls in 14 days: this is a success case. Come in expecting a testimonial.
If they've had 4–6 calls: habit is forming but not sticky. Come in focused on what's in the way.
If they've had fewer than 4: potential churn risk. Adjust to recovery mode.

---

## Opening (1 min)

> "Hey [name] — thanks for the time. Two weeks in. I just want to understand what's actually changed, if anything."

---

## Section 1 — The behavior shift (5 min)

**"Looking back at the last two weeks — has anything actually changed about how you start your mornings or think about your priorities?"**

This is the core question. You're not asking if they liked it. You're asking if anything changed.

Listen for:
- Specific behavioral shifts ("I now look at my priorities before opening email")
- Attitudinal shifts ("I feel less anxious at the start of the day")
- Increased intentionality ("I declined two meetings this week that I would have accepted before")

Any of these is a win. "Not really" needs follow-up.

**If something changed:**
> "Can you give me a specific example from this week?"

Make them be specific. The story behind the change is your strongest product evidence.

**If nothing changed:**
> "What would have needed to happen for it to feel like something shifted? Was there a moment where you expected it to help and it didn't?"

---

## Section 2 — What's become normal (3 min)

**"Do you think about the morning call now as something you just do, or does it still feel optional?"**

The answer tells you where they are on the habit curve:
- "I just do it" = habit forming → potential 30-day retainer
- "It still feels like something I try to remember" = reminder problem, not product problem
- "It still feels like a test" = not converted yet; needs a more compelling win before day-30

**"Has Edge ever said something that changed what you actually did that day?"**

Specific examples here are gold. "Edge said I'd been avoiding [thing] and asked me what was getting in the way. I had the conversation that afternoon." — that's a story that closes investors.

---

## Section 3 — Accuracy check (3 min)

**"By now Edge has had two weeks of calls with you. Does it feel like it knows you, or does it still feel generic?"**

You're listening for: does personalization feel like it's working? Does Edge get their context right?

**"Is there something important about you or your situation that Edge still seems to be missing?"**

This is your best product feedback. If Edge keeps treating them as if they're in one mode (e.g., pure executor) when they're actually in a transition (e.g., shifting from operator to fundraiser), that's a personalization gap. Log it for Core.

---

## Section 4 — The honest question (3 min)

**"Are you going to keep doing this at day 30?"**

Watch the delivery. A confident "yes" with a reason is different from "I think so."

If they say they'll continue:
> "What would make you stop? What would break the habit?"

Understanding the fragility of the habit is as important as knowing it's there.

If they're uncertain:
> "What would need to be different for this to be something you can see doing for 90 days?"

---

## Section 5 — Testimonial ask (3 min, only if the call warrants it)

You'll feel when this moment is right. If they've described a specific win, a meaningful shift, or said something vivid — ask.

> "Something you said there — '[their words]' — that's exactly the kind of thing I'd love to be able to share when we launch publicly. Would you be okay with me using something like that? I can keep it first-name only if you prefer."

See `content/testimonial-capture-guide.md` for what to do if they say yes.

If they're not there yet:
> "I'll probably ask you about a month in — by then the story will be clearer for both of us."

Don't push. A reluctant testimonial is worse than none.

---

## Close (2 min)

> "This has been genuinely helpful. Two things before I let you go:"

> "One — is there anything you want to ask me? About the product, where we're going, anything?"

> "Two — if there's someone in your network who's a high-performer and you think they'd benefit from this, I'd love an intro. We're small and intentional right now — I'm only taking 5 people, and a vouched intro from someone I already trust goes a long way."

That second ask is a soft referral request. Don't force it — let it land naturally.

---

## After the call — update `content/beta-user-feedback.md`

Add to the user's Day-14 section:
- Habit status: `sticky` / `forming` / `at-risk` / `churned`
- Call count: total / week 1 / week 2
- Behavioral shift: describe in one sentence or "none observed"
- Best quote from this call (verbatim)
- Testimonial status: `captured` / `asked (pending)` / `not yet`
- Referral: did they offer an intro?
- Day-30 prediction: will this person still be taking calls at day 30? Yes / Uncertain / No

---

## Interpretation

| Signal | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Habit sticky + behavioral shift + good quote | Full success — this user is your first case study | Capture testimonial, invite next user, use this story in angel outreach |
| Habit forming + no strong shift yet | Normal trajectory — week 3 often delivers the breakthrough | Continue; do a brief day-21 text check-in |
| Still optional + no behavioral shift | Product isn't earning the habit yet | Schedule a deeper conversation about what's actually in the way |
| Fewer calls in week 2 than week 1 | Habit regressing — act now, not at day-30 | Proactive text: "I noticed the calls trailed off — is anything getting in the way?" |
| Churn | Product-market fit miss for this person | Exit gracefully, extract signal: which ICP assumptions were wrong? |
