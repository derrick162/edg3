# Pitch Deck Outline — Edg3 Seed Round
_For angel conversations starting August 2026. Build the deck in Figma or Notion when you have retention data._
_Length: 10–12 slides. No more. Angels don't read 25-slide decks._

---

## The principle

The deck supports the conversation — it doesn't replace it. A great deck gives the investor something to read AFTER you've talked, to confirm what they already believe. Build for the re-read, not the first read.

Every slide should be answerable by a high-schooler. If it takes 30 seconds to understand a slide, cut it.

---

## Slide structure

### Slide 1 — Title + hook (what you leave up while you talk)

> **Edg3**
> The AI that calls you every morning and keeps you from burning out.
>
> Derrick Fung | Seed round | [Month] 2026

Keep it minimal. The hook does the work.

---

### Slide 2 — The problem (personal, specific)

**One image or number.** Not a market size graph.

Option A: a quote from a real founder about losing track of their priorities.
Option B: a visual showing how a "focused" executive's week actually looks (color-coded by priority vs not).
Option C: a one-sentence stat: "The average executive spends 23% of their week on their top priority. The rest goes elsewhere."

Then: "Most high-performers know what matters. They just can't stay connected to it while the day happens."

---

### Slide 3 — Your story (why you)

> "I co-founded TuneIn. Scaled to $100M+. Burned out — not once but slowly, over years. When I stepped back, I realized I'd never had a system that kept me connected to what actually mattered. I built Edg3 to be that system."

One paragraph. One photo (you, not a logo). Investors back people; this slide is about you.

---

### Slide 4 — The product (show, don't tell)

Two-column:
- LEFT: a screenshot of the call summary (what the user sees after a call)
- RIGHT: a screenshot of the day plan card (before/after)

Caption: "Edge calls you every morning. 10 minutes. Knows your calendar, your priorities, your energy. Reshapes your day with one tap."

Below: the Edge Score — "Measures how aligned your week actually is. Goes up when you act on what matters."

---

### Slide 5 — Traction (the retention number)

**One big number.** Whatever your day-7 retention is: "X/5 design partners still taking daily calls at day 7."

If you have a behavior-change story (one quote from a user): put it here.

The rest: total calls made, Edge Score improvement data, call streak records.

Don't pad with fake metrics. If the traction is thin, say "early beta — these are the numbers" and let the story carry it.

---

### Slide 6 — Why voice (the non-obvious insight)

This is your differentiation argument.

> "Every productivity tool requires you to remember to use it. A call meets you where you are — in your morning routine, before your phone fills up with everyone else's priorities. Voice is the only modality that works for someone who's already in motion."

Below: "The call is a forcing function. Text is optional."

---

### Slide 7 — Business model

Clean table:

| Tier | Price | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Individual | $69/month | Daily calls, full dashboard, priority derivation |
| Annual | ~$590/year | ~15% discount |
| Enterprise (future) | TBD | Teams, usage-based |

LTV math: "$69/month × 12 = $828/year. At $99 CAC, payback < 2 months."

Keep this slide simple. Don't forecast 5-year revenue. Angels don't believe it.

---

### Slide 8 — The market (keep this brief)

One graph or number. Don't do the "our TAM is $200 billion" slide.

Better framing: "Our ICP is the 4M+ founders and executives at early/growth-stage companies in North America. If we capture 0.5% of that market at $69/month, that's $165M ARR."

Or: use the coaching market: "The executive coaching market is $15B/year in the US. Edge delivers the core accountability function of a coach at 1/500th the cost."

---

### Slide 9 — Why now

Three bullets:
1. Voice AI is good enough (Vapi + Claude enable real conversations)
2. Burnout is an acknowledged crisis for founders/executives (it's discussable now)
3. Calendar AI is the unlock (reading + reshaping time in real time is new)

---

### Slide 10 — The ask

> **Raising:** $500K–$1M seed / pre-seed
> **Use of funds:** 12 months of runway for Derrick + one senior hire (product or engineering)
> **Milestone:** 100 retained users by December 2026; unit economics validated; Series A setup

**Why now:** beta retention data in hand (August); product-market fit signal is clear; raising before we need it.

---

### Slide 11 — Team

Derrick Fung: TuneIn co-founder, $100M+ scale, founder-market fit.

[Advisor 1 name]: [their credibility — AI/product/revenue].
[Advisor 2 name]: [their credibility].

If you have any relevant investors from the advisory asks, name them here once they're confirmed.

---

### Slide 12 — Appendix: deep product (leave off main deck)

Move this to an appendix slide — available if asked.
- Full Edge Score components (Focus 30% + Energy 30% + Clarity 20% + Momentum 20%)
- Privacy architecture (data-control screen, Privacy Mode, what's encrypted)
- Technical stack (Vapi, Claude, Google Calendar, Whoop)
- Roadmap (Notifications, voice prosody, team features)

---

## Build order

Build slides 1, 3, 4, 5 first — those are the story. Fill in 6, 7, 8 once the story is solid. Slides 2 and 9 can be done last (they're supporting, not leading).

**Don't build the deck until you have slide 5 data (retention).** A deck without traction is a vibes deck. The conversation can carry vibes; the deck needs signal.

**Target build date:** August 1 (once 30-day retention data is in hand).
