## North Star for this period

**Arrive at day 90 with:**
- Runway extended (income added or burn reduced)
- Health visibly improving (weight, energy, sleep)
- Edg3 in controlled launch with real users and real signal
- Work capacity rebuilt — one thing done well per day, not ten things half-done

This is a recovery and foundation quarter, not a growth quarter. The growth comes after.

## What this plan is NOT

- It is not a sprint to recreate Cardify scale in 90 days
- It is not permission to add new trading positions
- It is not a license to take on more than 3 priorities at once
- It does not include a podcast, an Asia trip, an AI CFO pivot, or a new consulting practice this quarter

All of those can wait. The mandate for this quarter is narrow on purpose: protect what you have, build the foundation, prove one thing works.
