# Testimonial Capture Guide
_For wave-1 design partners — when to ask, how to ask, how to use it._

---

## The timing rule

Ask at the **moment of peak clarity** — right after a call where they said something like:
- "That's exactly what I needed to hear this morning."
- "Edge helped me realize I've been avoiding the thing that actually matters."
- "I can't believe how much time I was wasting on X."

That statement IS the testimonial. Your job is to capture it before it fades.

**Do not ask:**
- During onboarding (they don't have a story yet)
- At the end of a neutral call ("yeah it was good")
- After a bug or a miss (wait until the product earns it back)

**Target window:** Day 7–14, or right after any call where a genuine "aha" moment happens.

---

## The three-message ask

Send this by text or email, same day while the moment is fresh.

**Message 1 (natural ask — preferred):**
> "Something you said on the call stuck with me — '[quote their exact words back].' Would you be okay if I used that as a testimonial? Could be a one-liner for the launch. Takes 30 seconds if you're okay with it."

Most people say yes immediately. If they do, skip to capture.

**If they don't respond in 24h, Message 2:**
> "No pressure at all — whenever you have a sec. I could use the quote from earlier directly, or if you'd prefer to write something fresh, a sentence or two about how you've been using it would be great."

**If they want to write their own, Message 3 prompt:**
> "Anything that captures what's been useful — even just 'The morning calls help me start the day focused instead of scattered' is perfect. First name + role is all I need."

---

## What a strong testimonial looks like

Strong testimonials are specific and outcome-focused. They don't say "I love it." They say what changed.

**Weak:**
> "Edge is really useful and the AI is impressive." — A.B., Tech Founder

**Strong:**
> "I realized I was spending my first two hours every day reacting to other people's schedules instead of my own priorities. Edge made that visible in the first week." — [First name], [Role]

**Strong:**
> "I knew I was avoiding the fundraising conversations. Edge just said it directly on the first call and asked me what was in the way. I'd never had a tool do that." — [First name], [Founder]

**Strong:**
> "I went from dreading Monday mornings to looking forward to the call. It's the one thing that makes me feel like I know what the day is actually for." — [First name], [Role]

Pattern: **before/after contrast**, **specific behavior**, **real stakes**. No adjectives about the technology.

---

## Capture format

Once you have a testimonial (quote or written):

```
Date: [date]
User: [first name, last name optional, role, company — whatever they consent to]
Quote: "[exact words]"
Source: [call transcript / text / written email]
Permission: [yes — text on DATE / yes — verbal on call / pending]
Photo: [yes / no / not asked]
Use: [Product Hunt / landing page / demo deck / all]
```

Log this in `content/beta-user-feedback.md` under the user's section.

---

## Permission levels

Ask clearly what you can use the quote for:
- **"For a Product Hunt listing"** — most say yes easily; it's a known credibility context
- **"For the website"** — slightly higher bar; some want to see the page first
- **"With your full name"** — always ask; first name only is a fine fallback
- **"With a photo"** — nice to have; only ask if they're already enthusiastic

If someone says "just first name, no photo, Product Hunt only" — honor it exactly.

---

## Usage map

| Testimonial type | Use it here |
|---|---|
| Specific "aha" moment (e.g., realized I was avoiding X) | Product Hunt first comment (highest visibility) |
| Before/after habit contrast | Landing page hero section (wave-2 site) |
| Morning routine / focus | LinkedIn founding post reply comment |
| Trust / accuracy ("Edge got me right") | Demo deck slide 4 — social proof |
| General positive experience | Email footer, outreach signature (lower priority) |

---

## If a design partner gives a great quote but won't do a formal testimonial

That's fine. You can still:
1. Use the insight anonymously: "One founder told us: 'I'd never had a tool just tell me what I was avoiding.'" — no attribution needed
2. Use it to sharpen your own positioning copy (it's a signal about what the product actually does)
3. Note it in `content/beta-user-feedback.md` as an observation even if not attributable

---

## Realistic expectations for wave-1

- 5 design partners → target 2–3 usable testimonials
- 1–2 will be Product Hunt-ready (specific + permission)
- The other 1–2 will be "using internally for now" — that's still a reference, not a testimonial

You only need 2 strong ones to launch credibly. A Product Hunt launch with 2 real quotes from real founders beats 5 generic ones.
