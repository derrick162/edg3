# Tough Questions FAQ
_Keep this open during investor conversations, design partner calls, and press interviews._
_Answers written for honesty — the goal is trust, not spin._

---

## Product questions

**"How is this different from ChatGPT?"**
> "ChatGPT is a tool you go to. Edge comes to you — it calls you. The call is the forcing function. Most people can't maintain a daily habit with a chatbot because opening the app is optional. A phone call isn't. And Edge has your actual calendar and priorities — it's not a general assistant, it's specifically about keeping you aligned with what matters."

**"What if I don't want to talk in the morning?"**
> "That's actually a useful signal. Edge keeps the calls short — usually under 10 minutes. The habit is easier than it sounds because it fits into the morning routine you already have: brush teeth, make coffee, take the call. But if someone really can't build the habit, that's a real product problem I want to know about."

**"What if Edge says something wrong?"**
> "It does sometimes, especially early on. The transcript is reviewable, and facts Edge stores can be corrected or deleted in the app. When something's wrong, I want to know immediately — that's why I'm hands-on in the beta. We have grounding logic that corrects the most common STT errors (names, calendar events), but it's not perfect."

**"Is this HIPAA-compliant?"**
> "We're not in clinical use and we don't store medical data — the Whoop integration is optional and shows fitness metrics, not medical records. We do encrypt everything at rest. For enterprise use cases where compliance is a hard requirement, that's a conversation we'd need to have specifically."

**"Can my whole team use it?"**
> "Right now it's individual only — one account, one person's calendar, one set of priorities. Team features are on the roadmap but not in the beta. I'm deliberately not building for it yet until I've validated the individual loop."

## Business questions

**"What does it cost?"**
> "Design partners in the beta are free — you're doing me a favor by giving real feedback. When we open to wave 2, the plan is $69/month with a 7-day free trial. That's less than 2 hours with a good business coach."

**"How do you make money long-term?"**
> "Individual subscriptions first. The interesting long-term play is teams — chiefs of staff or executives at the same company using it, where the data and patterns can compound. But I'm not building for that yet. The individual habit has to be proven first."

**"Are you raising money?"**
> "I'm having early conversations now for an August/September close. The plan is $500K–$1M pre-seed after I have 30-day retention data from the beta. I'm intentionally raising late — I'd rather come with signal than a story."

**"What's your runway?"**
> "I have enough runway to run the beta and get to product-market fit signal. I'm not raising out of desperation — I'm raising because the right money with the right angels accelerates the go-to-market by 12 months. The raise is strategic, not survival."

**"Why do you need money if you're the only employee?"**
> "The biggest thing money buys is time to focus. Right now I'm doing everything — engineering, support, sales, product. The right hire (a senior product engineer, or eventually a growth person) doubles our speed. And having angels means better intros for the Series A."

## Competitive/skeptic questions

**"Why won't Reclaim or Motion just add this?"**
> "They might. But calendar scheduling and daily coaching are fundamentally different product philosophies. Reclaim optimizes your calendar automatically — it's AI-as-background-process. Edge has an opinion about what you should be doing and tells you. Those require very different trust relationships with users. Being opinionated and challenging is a feature that's hard to add to a utility tool."

**"Isn't this just a notification? Why a phone call?"**
> "I tried notifications. They get snoozed. The call is a hard interrupt — it creates a moment. The five minutes after picking up are the most intentional part of a high-performer's morning, because they're not multitasking. That's where insight actually lands."

**"What happens when you lose access to Vapi or Claude?"**
> "We'd rebuild on another voice layer — the call infrastructure is a commodity, and Claude can be swapped for another model. The moat is the personalization data (calendar patterns, preference facts, energy history) and the habit. Those don't live in the infrastructure."

**"This seems hard to scale beyond 100 users — you can't be hands-on forever."**
> "That's exactly why the beta is structured the way it is. I'm investing heavily in each of the first 5 users to understand what 'success' looks like — what habits form, what breaks. By the time I'm at 100 users, the product should be carrying most of that weight. The white-glove phase is research, not the business model."

## Personal questions

**"Why are you building this instead of going back to operating something bigger?"**
> "Burnout reset my relationship with ambition. I'm not avoiding scale — I'm going after it differently. Building Edg3 lets me work on a problem I understand from the inside, at a pace that lets me think clearly. The irony is that this product is what I needed when I was building TuneIn."

**"Isn't this too niche? Just founders and executives?"**
> "That's 4 million people in North America alone. But more importantly — the patterns behind it (misalignment, reactive vs intentional work, energy vs effort) apply to anyone who leads. I'm starting where the pain is highest and the willingness to pay is clear. That's not a niche, it's a beachhead."
