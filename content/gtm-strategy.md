## The one-line pitch

> **Edge is the AI chief of staff that organizes your calendar around your focus and your energy — so you get more of what matters done, without burning out.**

This is the positioning. Everything below serves it.

---

## The problem we're solving (the honest version)

High performers — founders, execs, people with ADHD, anyone managing a full life at high velocity — are drowning in their own calendars. The calendar is full. The right things aren't getting done. The energy is running out. And most tools make it worse: another app, another list, another thing to manage.

The insight: **the calendar is already the operating system.** Everyone uses it. The problem isn't the tool — it's that nobody's helping them use it well. Edge doesn't ask you to adopt a new system. It upgrades the one you're already living inside.

---

## ICP — who we're building for

**Primary: the overwhelmed high-performer at risk of burnout.**
- Founder, exec, senior IC, or ambitious operator
- Calendar is full; priorities feel unclear or perpetually delayed
- High output but unsustainable — running on fumes, not strategy
- Already uses Google Calendar; lives and dies by it
- "I know what matters. I just can't seem to get to it."

**Strong sub-segment: high-performers with ADHD.**
- Prioritization, time-blindness, decision fatigue, energy dysregulation — Edge addresses all four
- "Do as little as possible, I'll handle the calendar" removes the friction that kills ADHD follow-through
- Loyal, vocal, underserved by productivity tools that assume neurotypical executive function
- Word-of-mouth velocity: ADHD communities share what actually works

**Secondary: the ambitious person building a life, not just a career.**
- Weight goals, financial goals, relationship health alongside professional ambition
- Wants a tool that sees the whole person, not just the work calendar
- Derrick is the prototype

## Why Edge wins

### 1. It does the work
Every other tool asks you to manage it. Edge manages for you. You do a 3-minute call. Your week gets reshaped. One "yes." Done.

### 2. Voice-first is the right interface
Nobody has time to open another app and input data. The morning call takes the same time as brushing your teeth and gives you a chief of staff who knows your priorities, your energy, and your schedule. The interface is conversation.

### 3. The Edge Score is sticky
A number that represents "how set up am I to crush it today" — with a breakdown showing exactly why and exactly what to change — creates a daily habit loop that competitors don't have. You check your score like you check your recovery. It becomes part of the morning ritual.

### 4. Burnout prevention is a real market
Calendar tools optimize logistics. Productivity tools optimize output. Almost nothing optimizes for sustainable energy. That gap is the opening — and it's growing as burnout becomes a mainstream conversation, not a fringe one.

### 5. Founder-market fit
Derrick has lived this story. Cardify, the burnout, the rebuild. That's not just a founder narrative — it's proof of concept. The target user is Derrick six months ago.

## Pricing philosophy

Edge should be priced as a **professional tool**, not a consumer app. The alternative framing: a part-time EA costs $2,000–5,000/month. Edge delivers the core value proposition of a chief of staff for a fraction of that.

**Suggested positioning:**
- Free trial: first 14 days (enough time to experience the hero loop meaningfully)
- Pro: $49–79/month (individual, full features)
- Consider annual: ~$499/year (saves ~2 months)

Do not launch at $9.99/month. It signals the wrong product and attracts the wrong users.
