# Edge — Content Calendar: July–August 2026
_Phase 2 GTM: build waitlist to 500–1,000 before public launch. Educational, not promotional._
_Posting cadence: 2–3x/week on LinkedIn + 3–4x/week on Twitter/X. 1–2 hours total/week._

---

## The content strategy

**What works:** Specific, personal, useful. Derrick's voice, not marketing copy.

**The formula:** One real observation about focus, energy, or burnout → one concrete thing → tie to what Edge does (soft, not hard sell). 80% educational, 20% product.

**What to avoid:** "Excited to announce," features without context, generic productivity advice, anything that sounds like a newsletter.

**The goal by August 31:** 500+ waitlist signups. 3+ design partners generating testimonials. Founding story published.

## Quick LinkedIn post format

Most LinkedIn posts that work follow this structure:

```
[One specific observation or question — 1 line]

[2–3 sentence development — personal, specific]

[The insight or the turn]

[One closing question to the reader]
```

Short > long. Specificity > generality. Questions get comments; comments get reach.

---

## Quick Twitter format

Single tweets: one observation, under 200 characters. No hashtags.
Threads: max 5 tweets. Lead with the most surprising thing.
Never: "excited to share," "as I always say," "here's a thread."
