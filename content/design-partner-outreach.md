## Who to target

**Ideal design partner profile:**
- Founder, executive, or ambitious operator — calendar-driven, clearly overwhelmed
- Someone you know personally (trust accelerates honest feedback)
- ADHD high-performers especially valuable — the product was built for them
- Not someone who needs convincing that AI can help — someone who already believes it and hasn't found the right tool

**Shoot for 5–8 people.** Enough to get signal, small enough to give real attention to.

## Outreach message — direct / personal (recommended)

**Subject:** want to try something I built?

**Body:**
Hey [NAME],

I've been building something I think you'll actually find useful — an AI chief of staff that calls you every morning, tells you what to focus on that day, and reshapes your calendar around it. The call takes about 5 minutes.

[PERSONALIZED LINE — e.g.: "I know you've mentioned feeling like your weeks get away from you" / "Given what you've got going on with [company/project], I think you'd get a lot out of this" / "You've always been my most honest critic, so I want you to try it"]

I'm letting a handful of people in before we open more broadly. No cost, no commitment — I just want people who'll actually use it and tell me what's wrong with it.

If you're in, I'll set you up this week. Takes about 5 minutes to get started.

Derrick

## Onboarding message (once they say yes)

**Subject:** You're in — here's how to get started

Hey [NAME],

Excited to have you trying Edge.

Here's all you need:
1. Go to [APP_URL] and create your account
2. Connect your Google Calendar (takes 30 sec)
3. Set your 3 areas of focus and pick your morning call time
4. Edge will call you tomorrow morning

The first call will feel a little rough — Edge doesn't know you yet. Give it 3–5 calls before you judge it. That's when it starts to feel like it actually knows how you work.

I'll check in after your first call. Any weirdness, just reply here.

Derrick

---

## Check-in after first week

**Subject:** how's Edge been?

Hey [NAME],

You've had a week with Edge — wanted to check in.

A few things I'm curious about:
- Does the morning call feel useful or does it feel like another thing?
- Is the Edge Score making sense?
- Anything that's felt off or annoying?

Be brutal. I'd rather know what's broken than have you quietly stop using it.

Derrick

---

## Design partner expectations (tell them upfront)

When someone says yes, set these expectations:
- You're in early — things will break or feel rough sometimes
- I want honest feedback, not encouragement
- I may call or message you directly to ask follow-up questions
- Your feedback directly shapes what gets built next
- This is a reciprocal thing — I'll give you full access, forever, and your name goes in the credits if you want it
