## The one-liner

**Edg3 is an AI chief of staff that calls high-performers every morning and keeps them focused on what actually matters — before the day takes over.**

---

## The problem

High-performers — founders, executives, operators — don't burn out from too much work. They burn out from misalignment: spending energy on the wrong things, losing sight of their real priorities under an avalanche of reactive demands.

The tools that exist today (to-do apps, productivity frameworks, calendars) require the user to maintain their own system. Most people can't. The system collapses under the weight of the day, usually by Tuesday.

---

## What Edg3 does

Every morning at a time the user sets, **Edge calls them** — voice call, 10 minutes. Edge knows their calendar, their priorities, and their recent energy levels. It briefs them on the day, surfaces what they've been avoiding, and proposes concrete changes to make the day align with what actually matters.

After the call, the app shows a reshaped day plan. One tap applies it — moving meetings, creating focus blocks, clearing the clutter. The **Edge Score** (a 4-component health score: Focus, Energy, Clarity, Momentum) tracks whether the user is spending their time in a way that matches their stated goals.

The calls get more personalized over time. After two weeks, Edge knows the user's patterns, their energy peaks and troughs, their recurring avoidances. It becomes the kind of advisor most high-performers can't afford.

## The founding story

Derrick Fung co-founded TuneIn and helped scale it to $100M+ in revenue. He burned out. Not dramatically — gradually, over years, while looking perfectly productive from the outside.

When he stepped back, he realized he'd never had a system that kept him connected to what was actually important. He'd had calendars, to-do lists, coaches — but nothing that operated at the intersection of real-time data (what's on my calendar today?) and real context (what am I actually trying to do this quarter?).

Edg3 is what he built for himself. The closed beta launched June 2026.

---

## Why now

Three converging shifts:
1. **Voice AI is good enough.** The 10-minute morning call is possible — and actually useful — in a way it wasn't 18 months ago.
2. **The burnout conversation has changed.** High-performers are openly talking about unsustainable work patterns; the shame is gone.
3. **Calendar AI is the unlock.** The calendar is the one source of truth about how time is actually being spent. AI that can read and reshape it in real time is new.

## The market

Primary ICP: founders and executives at early/growth-stage companies — the 2–10 direct report range, where the bottleneck is the leader's focus, not headcount.

Long-term: anyone who leads — project managers, operators, consultants, coaches — where time allocation directly determines outcomes.

---

## The technology

- **Voice AI:** Vapi (voice layer); Anthropic Claude (reasoning)
- **Calendar:** Google Calendar (read + write — Core to the product)
- **Health data:** Whoop integration (optional; recovery affects pacing)
- **Privacy:** all data encrypted at rest; user-controlled retention; Privacy Mode option
