## Day-1 / day-7 / day-14 check-in cadence

| Day | Action | Script |
|---|---|---|
| T+0 (call done) | Text: "How'd that go?" | Freeform — listen first |
| Day 1 post-call | If churn signal: voice call within 24h | "Can we spend 10 min walking through it together?" |
| Day 7 | Schedule 20-min structured interview | `content/day-7-checkin-script.md` |
| Day 14 | Schedule 20-min structured interview | `content/day-14-checkin-script.md` |
| Weekly Monday | 10-min health check | `content/beta-weekly-health-check.md` |

## What to log in `content/beta-user-feedback.md`

After every touch point:

1. The exact words they used (verbatim — paraphrases lose signal)
2. Whether Edge did something right or wrong in the most recent call
3. The type of error (if any): wrong fact / wrong timing / wrong event / other
4. Effort required from the user to get value (low / medium / high)
5. A one-sentence read: "This person is on the path" / "At risk — needs intervention" / "Churning"
