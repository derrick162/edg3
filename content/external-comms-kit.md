## Part 2 — Journalist / Media Pitch

_For inbound press interest or direct outreach. Use when a story angle is clearly there._

### The one-sentence angle for journalists

> "A founder who burned out building a $100M startup has built an AI tool to prevent the next generation of high-performers from doing the same."

This is the angle. It's human, specific, and newsworthy. It's not "new AI productivity app."

---

### Email pitch template

**Subject:** Story angle — AI chief of staff built after $100M burnout

Hi [NAME],

I came across your piece on [relevant article they wrote]. Thought you might be interested in something I'm building.

Two years later, I've built Edge: an AI that calls you every morning, reads your calendar, and reshapes your day around what actually matters. It's not a productivity app — it's the chief of staff I wish I'd had before I burned out.

What makes it different: it calls you (you don't have to remember to open it), it takes action in your calendar (not suggestions — actual changes), and it shows its work (every fact and every action visible to the user).

We're in design partner beta. Day-7 retention for the first cohort is [X]. Average calls per week: [X]. 

Happy to do a briefing, walk you through a live demo, or arrange a design partner interview. Let me know if there's a story here.

Derrick Fung
Founder, Edge
derrick@edg3.ai

---

### Story angles that work

1. **Burnout prevention** — most coverage on burnout is "here's the crisis." The angle here is a founder building a product to close the gap. Tech + human story.

2. **ADHD founders** — underserved and growing category. Edge is specifically built for ADHD executive function. "The first productivity tool designed for how ADHD brains actually work."

3. **Voice AI for work** — Siri and Alexa didn't work for professionals. Why now? What changed? Edge is a case study for what voice AI can actually do when it's calendar-integrated and goal-aware.

4. **Trust problem in AI productivity** — why do people buy AI tools and then stop using them? Edge's trust-first architecture (everything visible, everything reversible) is a product design story.

5. **Founder-market fit** — Derrick is the user. He uses Edge daily. This is rare and verifiable — can share call data, score trends, actual behavior.

### Story angles that don't work

- "AI will replace productivity apps" — too generic
- Technical deep dives on how Vapi/Claude work — nobody cares
- "Series A fundraising" — not the story right now

## Part 3 — Product Hunt Launch Copy

_Target: September 2026. This is a draft — update based on design partner feedback before submission._

### Tagline (under 60 characters)

> Your AI chief of staff. Calls every morning.

### Description (under 260 characters for PH)

> Edge calls you every morning, reads your calendar, and reshapes your week around what matters most. 5 minutes. No app to open. Built by a founder who burned out so you don't have to.

### Longer description (for PH "About" section)

**What it is:**
Edge is a voice AI that calls you every morning, reads your calendar, tells you what to focus on, and makes the calendar changes you approve. The call takes 5–10 minutes. Your day gets better.

**What makes it different:**
- It calls you. You don't have to remember to open it.
- It acts. Not suggestions — actual calendar changes with your approval and one-click undo.
- It's honest. Every fact it has about you is visible in a "What Edge knows" tab. Every action it takes is logged. Nothing is hidden.
- It knows your energy. Integrates with Whoop to schedule demanding work when you're actually ready for it.

**Who it's for:**
Founders, executives, and ambitious high-performers who feel like their week is getting away from them. Especially effective for ADHD high-performers — Edge does the deciding so you don't have to.

**The backstory:**
I raised $100M, burned out building Cardify/Drop, and spent two years building the tool I wish I'd had. Edge is the result.
