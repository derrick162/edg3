A small rounded pill for status, categories, and counts — tinted with the semantic palette.

```jsx
<Badge variant="success">✓ Completed</Badge>
<Badge variant="pending">Calling</Badge>
<Badge variant="info">Founders</Badge>
<Badge variant="danger">Missed</Badge>
```

Variants: `info` (indigo, default — also used for tags/categories), `success`, `pending` (amber), `danger`. Pairs a leading glyph (✓, 📍) with a short label. Always fully rounded.
