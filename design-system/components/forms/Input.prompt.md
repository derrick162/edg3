Single-line text field with an inset fill, hairline border, and indigo focus ring; pass `label` and `hint` for the standard stacked form row.

```jsx
<Input label="Email" type="email" placeholder="you@example.com" />
<Input label="Phone number" type="tel" hint="US & Canada only. Edg3 calls you here every morning." />
```

Accepts all native `<input>` props (`type`, `value`, `onChange`, `placeholder`, `required`). Pair with `Textarea` for multi-line and `Select` for choices.
